SANDBOX PRESETS (experimental, made 30 Sep 2026)
Nothing here touches your main presets. Pick one from the ReShade preset list (open the Sandbox folder).

Pair each one with the launcher's Lighting / Weather option for the best result:

  S1_Dawn            Lighting: Dawn        misty, warm sun patches, warm fairy lights
  S2_Midday          Lighting: Mid day     drifting cloud shadows, calm crystals
  S3_GoldenDusk      Lighting: Dusk        long gold light, warm glints
  S4_NightWatch      Lighting: Night       no extra darkening, strong breathing tiberium, blasts light the ground
  S5_Thunderstorm    Weather: RainStorm    heavy rain, wet ground, lightning
  S6_TiberiumStorm   Weather: TibRainStorm green rain, green lightning
  S7_Winter          Lighting: Artic Day / Weather: Snow
  S8_Sandstorm       Weather: SandStorm

New shaders (in the menu):
  4_ Living Tiberium     breathing fields + slow fairy-light glints
  5_ Weather             clouds locked to the ground, mist, rain, snow, lightning
  6_ Blast light and heat explosions light the ground, heat shimmer over fire
Each has a Debug view if something looks wrong.
